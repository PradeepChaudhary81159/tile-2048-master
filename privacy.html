Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026
App: Tile 2048 Master (Android)

This Privacy Policy describes how PC AppStudio (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile application Tile 2048 Master (the “App”). By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

Quick summary: Game progress stays on your device. Optional Google Sign-in is used only for cloud backup. Ads (Google AdMob) and optional in-app purchases may process limited device/account data. We do not sell your personal information.

1 Information stored on your device

The App stores data locally on your device (using Android SharedPreferences and/or a local database), including:

  • Game progress (board state, score, moves, level, grid size)
  • High scores, statistics, and game history
  • Daily / weekly challenge progress and streaks
  • Achievements unlock status
  • Theme, accent color, sound, and vibration preferences
  • Power-up usage (undo, remove, shuffle)
  • Optional local player name for leaderboards
  • Whether the “Remove Ads” purchase is active
  • Daily reminder preference (on/off)

This data remains on your device unless you use optional cloud backup or clear app storage.

2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App may display ads served by Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads (for example, extra undos or continue after game over).

Google AdMob and related Google services may collect or process:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location (derived from IP)
  • Device model, OS version, and app information
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

For details, please review:
Google Privacy Policy · AdMob & Privacy

You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (Google Settings → Ads, or your system’s privacy controls). You may also remove ads permanently via the optional in-app purchase.

3 In-app purchases

The App offers an optional “Remove Ads” purchase processed by Google Play Billing. Purchase status is verified with Google Play and stored locally (and optionally synced if you use cloud backup).

Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Google’s terms and privacy policy apply to purchases.

4 Optional Google Sign-in & cloud backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, we may process basic account identifiers (such as Google account ID, display name, and email) solely to:

  • Back up and restore game progress, scores, achievements, and settings
  • Sync data across your devices

Sign-in is completely optional. You can use all core features of the App without an account. Cloud data is linked to your account identifier and is not sold to third parties. Google Sign-in is also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

5 Notifications

With your permission, the App may send local notifications (for example, a daily challenge reminder). Notification permission is requested on Android 13+ and can be turned off at any time in system settings or in the App’s settings.

We do not send marketing push notifications from our own servers.

6 Crash reporting & stability (if enabled)

The App may use Firebase Crashlytics (or similar) to collect anonymous crash logs and diagnostic data so we can improve stability. This may include device model, OS version, and stack traces. It does not intentionally collect your name, email, or precise location.

7 Permissions

  • Internet — ads, optional cloud sync, opening links (privacy policy, store pages)
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback during gameplay (can be disabled in settings)
  • Post notifications — optional daily challenge reminders (Android 13+)

The App does not require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location.

8 Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9 Data retention

  • Local data: remains until you clear app storage, uninstall the App, or use in-app reset options
  • Ad-related data: retained by Google according to their policies
  • Cloud backup data: kept while your account is linked; you may request deletion by contacting us
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play as required for transactions

10 Your choices & rights

  • Uninstall the App or clear storage to remove local game data
  • Turn off personalized ads in system / Google settings
  • Disable vibration, sound, or daily reminders in App settings
  • Skip Google Sign-in if you do not want cloud features
  • Request access to or deletion of cloud-linked data by emailing us

Depending on your location (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights regarding your personal data. Contact us to exercise those rights.

11 Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Please keep your device updated and use a screen lock.

12 Third-party services

The App may use the following third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase (Crashlytics / related, if used)
